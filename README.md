# TrueWave Support

TrueWave is an offline binaural-beat and tone generator for iPhone and iPad.

## Get help

For bug reports, purchase or restore questions, and feature requests, please [open a support request](https://github.com/chengai1106/truewave-support/issues/new).

When reporting a problem, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

## Purchases

TrueWave uses Apple's In-App Purchase system. Purchases can be restored from the app's Settings screen. Billing and refund requests are handled by Apple.
