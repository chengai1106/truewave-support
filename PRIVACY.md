# TrueWave Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 20, 2026

TrueWave does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

## Data collection

TrueWave has no user accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or developer-operated servers. Audio is generated locally on your device. The app does not upload listening activity, custom frequencies, or usage history.

## Apple services

When you purchase or restore the one-time unlock, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. Apple's handling of purchase and account information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. TrueWave receives only the purchase entitlement needed to unlock features.

When you choose to rate the app, TrueWave opens Apple's App Store interface.

## Changes

If TrueWave's privacy practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change takes effect.

## Contact

For privacy questions, [open a support request](https://github.com/chengai1106/truewave-support/issues/new). Do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.
